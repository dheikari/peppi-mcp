# Draft inquiry — not sent

Subject: Supported read-only access to my own Peppi study data

Hello,

I am a University of Lapland student developing an unofficial local, read-only
connector to help me review my studies. Local import of a student-selected PDF
already works. I am now investigating live access, starting with study rights
and completed studies. I would like to clarify:

1. Is there an interface individual students may use to retrieve their own
   study rights, completed studies, enrolments and recorded HOPS? If so, where
   are its access conditions, authentication/scopes and request limits documented?
   Can an individual student register a local client, or is an institutional
   agreement/service account required? Is there a test environment or sample schema?
2. Are signed PDF transcripts the available export format, or is a structured
   export also supported? Is there a supported personal timetable/calendar feed?
3. May a student use an automated personal browser session for read-only access
   after normal HAKA/MFA login, and what conditions apply? If suitable, which
   personal read destinations and session-handling method should a local client use?
4. Is there a documented public study-guide API, or guidance for using its
   anonymous course/search/realization routes in an unofficial open-source tool?

The connector would not enrol, withdraw, edit plans or collect data in the
background. Each user would use their own access. Selected results could be
passed to the user's AI assistant provider, which would be explained explicitly.

Thank you.
